# Research limits after the documentation and identity review

Reviewed September 22, 2026. The largest institution-count discrepancies cannot be evaluated using one match percentage. An FSA reporting unit, an IPEDS institution, a component reporting parent and a published IPEDS universe are different units. The repaired files should expose those distinctions and quantify unresolved cases; matching every FSA row to one UNITID would conceal some real reporting relationships.

**What was exhaustively reviewed**

The source-schema inventory contains 1,515 header records from all 86 selected cached FSA workbooks. All 64 distinct source measure names have exact observed year sets in the measure metadata. The 48-event register distinguishes 42 confirmed policy events, three schema changes with unresolved causes, two schema observations and one source-explained schema change. Every event has a source URL. All four policy-registry tests pass, including the comparison of metadata year sets against the complete selected-header inventory. [Source inventory](../Metadata/source_schema_inventory.csv), [event register](../Metadata/program_policy_events.csv), [policy review and official sources](policy_and_reporting_changes.md).

The IPEDS review covers 53 distinct dictionary vintages, with 49 selected annual dictionaries: HD1999–2025 and FLAGS2004–2025. Its 916 field entries retain annual definitions, codes, URLs, hashes and archive members. This includes the refreshed 2023/2024 dictionaries and the partial 2025 FLAGS release. It is exhaustive for the listed identity, activity, universe and reporting-relationship fields in those files. It does not claim that every IPEDS component's full questionnaire, every institutional merger record or every Title IV statute has been encoded. [Annual coverage and findings](ipeds_documentation_review.md), [field evidence](evidence/ipeds_annual_dictionary_evidence.json).

**Institution matching and count discrepancies**

| Issue | Verified conclusion and remaining research implication |
| --- | --- |
| Identifier normalization | Retain the complete eight-digit OPEID, including the two-digit location suffix. An OPEID root, an eight-digit OPEID, a Federal School Code and a UNITID are different identifiers. Padding a Federal School Code is unsafe: the official 2001–02 FAFSA list gives Quapaw code 010848, while annual HD gives full OPEID 02120500. [HD2004 definition](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/HD2004_Dict.zip), [official Federal School Code list, Arkansas](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED446591.pdf). |
| Identity versus reporting scope | A verified site identity does not prove that the FSA amount covers only that site. Official crosswalk primary/additional matches and parent references must remain separate. A one-to-many reporting unit must not duplicate its whole dollar amount onto every site. Crosswalk Source 3 carries a relationship from subsequent years; it warrants a historical-relation flag, even when its UNITID is verified in annual HD. [Official archive, Crosswalks workbooks and their notes](https://ed-public-download.scorecard.network/downloads/College_Scorecard_Raw_Data_06102026.zip). |
| Component parents | Finance partial/full children, SFA children and enrollment children have different meanings. Finance allocation factors do not allocate FSA aid. Preserve each `PRCH_*`/`IDX_*` pair, missing-parent references and unavailable component metadata. The revised scope enumeration rejects undocumented codes instead of treating any nonnegative value as known scope. [FLAGS2016](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/FLAGS2016_Dict.zip), [full FLAGS2024](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/FLAGS2024_Dict.zip). |
| Combined FISAP reporting | Separately eligible institutions with different OPEIDs can file one FISAP under common control/ownership. FSA's March 2013 instructions clarified that the former “Additional Locations” label had caused incorrect reporting and changed it to “Additional Institutions.” This does not date the beginning of combined reporting or quantify historical errors. A six-digit root therefore does not fully define FISAP coverage. The same announcement explains first-digit overflow for more than 99 locations, reinforcing the need to preserve complete OPEIDs. [2013 FSA clarification](https://fsapartners.ed.gov/fsa-print/publication/7778). |
| Activity and participation | `ACT=M` retains a closed institution with reportable prior-year data; `ACT=G` identifies a reporting child. Neither automatically invalidates a historical identity. `OPEFLAG=2` identifies a participating branch; code 6 means nonparticipation without an OPEID, not loss of eligibility. Codes 4/8 vary with historical universe timing. These classifications should be research flags, with annual definitions. [Annual dictionary review](ipeds_documentation_review.md). |
| Official documentation conflict | Scorecard's September 2025 documentation groups `OPEFLAG=8` as non-Title-IV, while annual HD defines it as a new spring participant. The discrepancy remains unexplained. Preserve raw annual HD codes and use the corresponding annual dictionary; Scorecard's derived category is not a universal historical eligibility rule. [Scorecard documentation, page 6](https://collegescorecard.ed.gov/files/InstitutionDataDocumentation.pdf), [annual comparison](ipeds_documentation_review.md). |
| Denominator and geography | Compare unique UNITIDs within an explicitly selected IPEDS universe and geography, separately from FSA reporting-row counts. HD includes administrative, inactive and nonparticipating records; FSA includes foreign schools outside the domestic IPEDS universe. Modern First Look definitions use `PSET4FLG` 1 and 3, while earlier reports use 1. An unmatched IPEDS record does not establish missing FSA aid. [HD2024 universe definitions](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/HD2024_Dict.zip). |
| Reference year and institutional change | HD collection year is not a universal reference period for Finance, SFA and enrollment. Compare award-year-start/end anchors and preserve identifier changes. `NEWID`, deletion year and actual closure date are distinct evidence; none alone authorizes moving all historical aid to a successor. [HD2024](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/HD2024_Dict.zip). |
| Missing source identifiers | The frozen review approves 34 full-OPEID repairs, restoring $29,648,694.58 in Pell across existing institution-year keys. Another 16 grant rows have verified UNITID component identities but unresolved original FSA OPEIDs, covering $151,082,578.97 in Pell. They require separate, collision-checked UNITID inclusion with null OPEID. Sixteen grant rows with $9,015,765.83 in Pell and 21 zero-only Campus-Based rows still lack approved identities. Their uncertainty must remain visible in coverage counts and dollar exclusions. [Evidence ledger](../Metadata/source_identity_resolutions.csv), [case review and official sources](source_identity_recovery.md). |

Eight identity-resolution tests pass. They check same-award-year independent evidence, stale source hashes/sheets/rows, changed raw descriptors, collision rejection, preservation of every original measure cell and the distinction between verified UNITID and reconstructed OPEID. These are evidence-contract tests; they do not establish site-level allocation of shared aid. Final panel counts and conservation checks must come from the actual completed build, rather than from this documentation review.

**Policy and measurement limits that remain material**

The reviewed policy register covers every observed measure-family addition, removal and split in the selected files. Several apparently inconsistent boundaries are legitimate: July 2006 Grad PLUS processing explicitly supports AY2005–06; the separate IASG program moves into Pell for AY2024–25; and Perkins federal capital, student lending, final disbursement and header removal end at different times. Treat effective dates separately from observed column windows. [Grad PLUS operational guidance](https://fsapartners.ed.gov/knowledge-center/library/electronic-announcements/2006-06-23/summary-hera-operational-implementation-guidance-cps-cod-system-and-edexpress-suite-updated-guidance-direct-plus-loan-graduate-and-professional-students-supersedes-5162006-electronic), [IASG/Pell transition](https://fsapartners.ed.gov/knowledge-center/library/electronic-announcements/2024-03-15/pact-act-impacts-iraq-and-afghanistan-service-grants-iasg-eligibility-and-processing-2023-24-award-year-updated-pell-grant-eligibility-dependents-certain-deceased-servicemembers-and-public), [Perkins wind-down](https://fsapartners.ed.gov/knowledge-center/fsa-handbook/2024-2025/vol6/ch3-participating-perkins-loan-program).

The following gaps remain genuine research restrictions:

- The widespread Direct Loan dash convention from 2017, the exact reason for the 2016 Perkins allocation-column removal and the publication-policy origin of Campus-Based suppression from 2021 remain unverified. Preserve the source symbols and unresolved-cause labels. A literal `<10` supports conservative integer bounds 0–9; it does not identify the suppressed dollar amount.
- Only 76 of the 86 selected workbooks contain definition sheets. Ten do not. Later semantics cannot be represented as direct quotations from missing sheets. Definitions are sometimes retrospective even within early-award-year files; a definition mentioning a later policy does not establish that the policy applied in that early year. [Workbook evidence](evidence/selected_workbook_schema_definitions.json).
- A complete annual mapping of published Campus-Based disbursements to individual FISAP lines remains unavailable. Federal allocations differ from total program payments, which can include transfers and nonfederal shares. CARES matching waivers further change comparability. Do not infer federal spending or campus allocation by a fixed multiplier. [FISAP instructions, Part VI](https://fsapartners.ed.gov/sites/default/files/attachments/fisapformandinst/0809FISAPInstructions.pdf), [campus accounting and waivers](https://fsapartners.ed.gov/knowledge-center/fsa-handbook/2022-2023/vol6/ch1-campus-based-programs-common-elements).
- The policy register does not supply a complete numeric annual Pell maximum, EFC/SAI schedule, appropriation, fee or inflation series. Continuous column names do not imply constant recipient eligibility or purchasing power. In particular, 2024–25 Pell changes need a separate policy regime in substantive analysis. [2024–25 SAI and Pell eligibility](https://fsapartners.ed.gov/knowledge-center/fsa-handbook/2024-2025/application-and-verification-guide/ch3-student-aid-index-sai-and-pell-grant-eligibility).
- Recipient counts overlap across programs, channels, institutions and years. Loan-channel aggregation is not debt-consolidation volume; historical definitions exclude consolidation loans. Q4 cumulative award-year values are selected once, and later revisions can change full-year totals. FSA explicitly warns that unique recipients across program types are unavailable at school level and initial reports can understate final activity. [FSA report interpretation](https://fsapartners.ed.gov/knowledge-center/library/electronic-announcements/2026-03-13/federal-student-aid-posts-updated-reports-fsa-data-center).
- The selected source inventory is an unbalanced observed-report panel, not a roster of all eligible institutions. Institution absence, unavailable report, missing header, blank, dash, suppression and numeric zero remain different states. The latest cached files' internal dates do not certify that no newer official release exists. Similarly, partial FLAGS2025 limits Finance/SFA scope review while permitting separately verified HD2025 identity. [FLAGS2025](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/FLAGS2025_Dict.zip), [HD2025](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/HD2025_Dict.zip).

**Follow-up search for the three unresolved schema causes**

The September 22 follow-up rechecked the boundary-year workbook definitions, available footer notes, FSA report-release announcements and official-domain searches for dash/zero, suppression/redaction, `<10`/less-than-ten and Perkins federal-award removal. None located an explicit explanation for these exact publication changes. This is a documented search result, not proof that no unpublished or unindexed explanation exists.

| Unresolved change | Sources checked and limit of their evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Direct Loan dashes from AY2017–18 | The selected 2016–17, 2017–18 and 2018–19 definition sheets explain exclusion of zero-dollar transactions from disbursement counts, but never equate a literal dash with zero. The [September 2017 release announcement](https://fsapartners.ed.gov/fsa-print/publication/8773) explains cumulative tabs and delayed reporting, not dash semantics. [Workbook definitions and source hashes](evidence/selected_workbook_schema_definitions.json). |
| Perkins federal-award column absent from AY2016–17 | The selected 2015–16/2016–17/2017–18 definitions retain generic federal-award wording without explaining the removal. The [2016–17 funding announcement](https://fsapartners.ed.gov/knowledge-center/library/electronic-announcements/2016-03-28/campus-based-subject-2016-2017-final-funding-authorizations-campus-based-programs) confirms zero new Perkins capital while separately retaining authorized revolving-fund expenditure. It does not state why this particular volume-report column disappeared in 2016. The 2019–20 through 2022–23 workbook footers explain the later removal of recipient/disbursement columns only. |
| Campus-Based suppression from AY2021–22 | Rechecked selected AY2020–21 through AY2023–24 workbooks: their available footer notes do not establish the privacy publication rule or its adoption date, and they lack definition sheets. The [December 2021 release announcement](https://fsapartners.ed.gov/fsa-print/publication/1003632) describes separate FISAP-derived program data books without specifying this threshold. The [FSA-published dataset catalog](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/campus-based-program-data-for-federal-student-aid) describes annual recipient/disbursement/award coverage but supplies no suppression methodology. |

An unrelated convention was explicitly rejected as evidence: the [2024–25 COD Technical Reference, Volume 6](https://fsapartners.ed.gov/sites/default/files/2024-02/2425CODTechRefVol6.pdf) uses em dashes for no data in its own technical tables. Its field-layout and processing tables do not define the literal hyphens in historical public loan-volume spreadsheets. Likewise, generic FERPA/privacy requirements do not establish the date or exact threshold of this Campus-Based publication change. The public Title IV landing page returned no readable explanatory text in this retrieval; some direct FSA pages required indexed or print versions. No causal assertion or numeric recoding was added from these search gaps.

Research use should therefore state the institution universe, reporting unit, annual anchor, policy regime, release vintage and unresolved-dollar exclusions. A strict standalone-institution subset is a sensitivity sample; its institution count is not the total number of FSA institutions with valid IPEDS identities.
